# Team Charter — Capstone Project

**Team members:** Eslam, Ayman, Ali  
**Project:** Enterprise E-Commerce Platform  
**Effective date:** __________________

## Working agreement

- **Working hours:** The team agrees on working hours and meeting times at the start of each week. Members notify the team in advance if they cannot attend an agreed session.
- **Communication:** The team uses **[enter the agreed team communication channel]** for project updates and coordination. Urgent blockers are raised there promptly.
- **Pull requests:** Every change is submitted through a pull request (PR); no direct commits to `main`. Each PR receives at least one teammate review within 24 hours. Review feedback is addressed before merging.
- **Blockers:** A member blocked for more than one hour shares the issue, relevant error or context, what they have tried, and the help they need in the team channel. The team agrees on a next step and owner.
- **Disagreements:** The team discusses the options, evidence, trade-offs, and project impact. Significant architecture decisions are recorded in the Architecture Decision Document (ADD). If the team cannot reach agreement, the current Tech Lead makes the decision after hearing each member's view.
- **Rotating roles:** Tech Lead, DevOps/Security, and QA/Docs roles rotate at each gate. The team plans the rotation so every member holds each role at least once.
- **Respect and ownership:** Members communicate respectfully, share knowledge, keep teammates informed about task progress, and take responsibility for work they accept. Pair work and co-authored commits are recorded honestly.
